Privacy Policy

Candy Voyage · Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

Candy Voyage has no accounts, no login, and no servers of our own. We never receive your name, email, phone number or location.

Your game progress stays on your phone. Four outside services do receive limited technical data, and each is described below: Google AdMob (for ads), Unity Ads (for ads when AdMob has none), Google Firebase (for game statistics and game settings) and Google Play Billing (for purchases).

Who we are

Candy Voyage is published by Kyrac Studio. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. Questions: kyracstudiosupport@gmail.com

What stays on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your device's own app storage:

• Level progress, stars and scores

• Coins, boosters and lives

• Whether an ad-free pass is active, and when it expires

• Sound and music settings

• Daily-reward streak information

• Your privacy choices and the times of your reminder notifications

None of this is transmitted to us. We cannot see it, and we have no server that could store it. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, AdMob may collect:

• Your device's advertising identifier

• Device and operating-system information

• Approximate location derived from your IP address

• Which ads were shown and interacted with

This data goes to Google, not to us. It is governed by Google's own privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Doing so does not remove ads, but makes them less targeted.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choices in its own privacy window before any ads or statistics start. Unless you allow personalised ads, AdMob shows only non-personalised ads. You can change your choices at any time in the game's Settings → Privacy choices.

Unity Ads (advertising)

When Google AdMob has no ad to show, the app shows one through Unity Ads instead. To serve and measure those ads, Unity may collect:

• Your device's advertising identifier

• Device and operating-system information

• Approximate location derived from your IP address

• Which ads were shown and interacted with

This data goes to Unity, not to us. It is governed by Unity's own privacy policy at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Unity is given the same choice you make in the game's privacy window: unless you allow personalised ads, it shows only non-personalised ads. The Android ad settings described above apply to Unity's ads too.

Google Firebase (game statistics and settings)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how the game is played: for example which levels are started, won, lost or left, when a player runs out of lives, when a full-screen ad was shown, whether reminders and the update message are used, the highest level reached, which in-app purchases were made (the product and its price, never your payment details), and whether the app was later uninstalled.

These statistics are linked to a random ID that the app creates, together with basic device information (such as phone model, Android version and language) and an approximate country or region derived from your IP address. They are not linked to your name, email, phone number or advertising ID, and they are never used for advertising. We use them only to improve the game.

The app also uses Firebase Remote Config to download game settings, such as how often full-screen ads may appear, so these can be adjusted without an app update.

This data is processed by Google on our behalf under Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, game statistics are collected only if you allow "Game stats" in the game's privacy window, and Firebase sends nothing until you answer. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

Reminder notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules up to three reminders a day on your phone (for example, that a daily gift or full lives are waiting), for up to 7 days after you last played.

They are created on your device; no information is sent anywhere to show them. You can turn them off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Candy Voyage → Notifications.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

Coin packs and the ad-free pass are sold through Google Play. Google processes the entire payment.

We never see, receive or store your card number, UPI ID, bank details or billing address. We are only told that a purchase for a given product completed, so the app can grant the item.

Your purchase history is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Children

Candy Voyage is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app is listed for a general audience aged 13 and over.

Your choices and rights

We hold no account, name or contact details for you. Game statistics are tied only to a random app ID (see Google Firebase above), so we cannot tell which statistics are yours. To remove everything the app has stored and start with a new random ID, uninstall it, or use Android Settings → Apps → Candy Voyage → Storage → Clear data.

For data held by Google, use Google's own privacy controls at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy

For data held by Unity, use the choices described in Unity's privacy policy (linked above).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and the date below will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
